Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18/11/2025

WiFi attendance (“the App”) is developed by Mantu Ali.
This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

1. Information We Collect

The App does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information.

Depending on the app features, it may use:

Camera (for scanning QR codes or flashlight function)

Location / WiFi info (only used inside the device to detect WiFi networks)

Internet (for ads only)

No data is uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Permissions

The App uses permissions only for core features:

Camera Permission:
Used only for scanning QR codes or controlling flashlight.

Location/WiFi Permission:
Used to scan nearby WiFi networks for attendance features.
We do not collect or track your location.

Internet Permission:
Used to load ads (AdMob).
Ads may collect anonymous usage data.

3. Third-Party Services
